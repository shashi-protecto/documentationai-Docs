---
title: Cannot Access Dashboard
description: Fix issues accessing the Protecto dashboard.
---

## Symptoms

- Login page does not load
- Error: "Unable to connect to server"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your internet connection">
    Open another website to verify connectivity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try reloading `https://dashboard.protecto.com` after clearing cache.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try incognito/private mode">
    Some browser extensions can block the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support">
    If issue persists, email us: support@protecto.com
  </Step>
</Steps>
