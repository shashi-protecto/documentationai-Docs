---

## Quick Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login to Protecto">
    Go to `https://dashboard.protecto.com` and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Project">
    Click the "Create Project" button on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Project Details">
    Fill in the project name, description, and select a workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Project">
    Click "Save". Your project appears on the dashboard and documentation can begin.
  </Step>
</Steps>
