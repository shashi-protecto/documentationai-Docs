## Getting Started

To begin using Protecto:

1. Sign up at `https://dashboard.protecto.com`
2. Verify your email address
3. Log in to your Protecto dashboard
4. Click "Create Project" to start documenting

## Tip

<Callout kind="info">
Protecto lets you organize, manage, and collaborate on all your project documentation in one place.
</Callout>
